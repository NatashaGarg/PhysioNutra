Frequently Asked Questions

Everything international patients ask us about online consultations.

How does an online physiotherapy consultation with PhysioNutra work?

You book a time slot in your own timezone, choose a video platform, and pay the consultation fee to confirm. Dr. Tarun Garg then conducts a full assessment over video call, discusses your condition, and provides a personalised home exercise and treatment plan.

How much does an online physiotherapy consultation cost?

A standard online consultation is USD 35, booked at least 24 hours in advance. A same-day or emergency consultation is USD 50. The amount is billed in USD via a secure payment link; this page shows an estimate in your local currency based on current exchange rates.

Is online physiotherapy as effective as in-person treatment?

For assessment, exercise prescription, and rehabilitation guidance, yes — video consultations work very well. Assessment happens through movement screening, guided self-tests, and detailed history. If your condition needs hands-on treatment, we will tell you honestly that an in-person visit would serve you better.

Which countries do you provide online physiotherapy consultations for?

We consult with patients anywhere in the world, subject to timezone availability. Our booking calendar runs 9:00 AM – 11:00 PM IST and automatically converts every slot to your local timezone.

What do I need for an online physiotherapy session?

A camera device, roughly 2 metres of clear space, comfortable clothing, and any reports or scans you already have. Your joining link arrives by email after booking.

Can Dr. Tarun Garg treat me remotely without a physical examination?

Online consultation focuses on assessment through movement screening, guided self-tests, and detailed history, followed by a personalised exercise and self-management plan. For conditions requiring hands-on treatment, we advise honestly if an in-person visit would serve you better.

What video platform is used for the consultation?

You choose when booking — Zoom, Google Meet, or WhatsApp Video.

Is online physiotherapy consultation suitable for NRIs visiting India?

Yes. Many of our online patients are NRIs who received treatment during a visit to India and want to continue guidance and progress reviews with the same physiotherapist after returning abroad.

Can I reschedule or cancel my online consultation?

Yes. Your confirmation email includes a link to reschedule or cancel. We ask for at least 6 hours' notice so the slot can be released to another patient.

How do I pay from outside India?

Right after booking you receive a secure payment link that accepts international debit and credit cards. The confirmed charge is in USD; your bank converts it to your local currency.
