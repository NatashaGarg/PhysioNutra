US Patient FAQs

How much does online physical therapy cost without insurance?

Our standard telehealth physical therapy session is a flat $35 USD, and same-day urgent sessions are $50 USD. This is significantly lower than the average US out-of-pocket cost or high-insurance deductible for in-clinic PT, which typically ranges from $150 to $300 per session.

Do you accept US health insurance (Medicare, Medicaid, or private)?

No, we do not bill US health insurance directly. We operate on a transparent, direct-pay cash model. Because our sessions are $35, many US patients find it more affordable to pay out-of-pocket rather than meeting a high annual deductible or paying high specialist copays.

Are your physical therapists licensed in the United States?

Our consultations are provided by Dr. Tarun Garg, a highly experienced Senior Physiotherapist based in India with over 10 years of clinical practice. While he is not licensed by individual US state boards (meaning we cannot bill US insurance or provide legal medical diagnoses for US workers' comp), he provides world-class, evidence-based rehabilitation guidance, exercise prescription, and second opinions.

What if I need hands-on manual therapy?

Modern physical therapy research shows that active rehabilitation (targeted loading and movement) is far more effective than passive manual therapy for long-term recovery. Through video, Dr. Tarun will guide you through specific self-mobilizations, targeted exercises, and movement modifications. If your condition strictly requires hands-on intervention, we will honestly advise you to seek a local manual therapist.

What do I need for a telehealth PT session?

A smartphone or laptop with a camera, comfortable clothing (shorts if we are assessing your knees/legs), and about 6 feet of clear floor space. If you have any MRI reports or X-rays, you can upload them directly when you book.
